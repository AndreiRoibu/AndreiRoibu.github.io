## Situation

Synthara provides the world's first commercially available silicon-proven in-memory computing (IMC) IP solutions. Synthara’s technology operates on the matrix-product primitive, unlocking significant speed and energy efficiency improvements. However, fully demonstrating the practical advantages of this novel technology required robust, optimized algorithms tailored explicitly for Synthara's IMC hardware.

As a Field Applications Engineer, my multifaceted role involved:

1. Developing and optimizing matrix-based algorithms (AI, cryptography, signal processing) to fully leverage Synthara’s IMC hardware.
2. Bridging customer requirements with internal engineering teams to ensure product-market alignment.
3. Support business development, marketing, and sales through technical demonstrations, customer engagements, and strategic insights.
