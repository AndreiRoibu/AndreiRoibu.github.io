## Situation

Accurate MRI segmentation is critical for Alzheimer's research, yet existing open-source tools struggled with the severe brain atrophy common in patients, and the lower-quality imaging data. Researchers and clinicians urgently needed a robust, accessible, and more accurate tool to accelerate their work in neurodegenerative disease.

<img src="/assets/images/p4b1.png" alt="difference-healthy-alzheimer" class="align-center" style="border-radius: 1%;">

## Task

My mission was to lead the integration of a state-of-the-art deep learning model into the **open-source Paint4Brains software**. The goal was to deliver a significantly faster and more accurate tool tailored for atrophic brains, making it a valuable asset for the global neuroimaging community.

## Action

My contribution involved three key roles:

* **AI Model Integration:** I evaluated, fine-tuned, and integrated the [QuickNat](https://github.com/ai-med/quickNAT_pytorch) model into the Paint4Brains backend, optimizing its performance for neurodegenerative disease-specific anatomies.
* **Project Management:** Using Agile methodologies, I managed the whole project lifecycle and was the key liaison between the technical development team and stakeholders at **GE Healthcare**.
* **Communication & Adoption:** I created comprehensive documentation and delivered training sessions to clinical and industry end-users, ensuring the successful adoption of the tool.

## Interdisciplinary Integration

This project required a synthesis of three key domains:

* **AI Research:** Selecting and fine-tuning advanced deep learning models for specific neuroimaging challenges.
* **Software Engineering:** Integrating the model into a robust, user-friendly, open-source application.
* **Stakeholder Management:** Aligning the technical development roadmap with the practical needs of clinical researchers at GE Healthcare.

## Result

The project was a clear success, delivering significant value to the research community:

* **Boosted segmentation accuracy by 12%** for brain regions affected by Alzheimer's, a critical improvement for clinical data. It also added, for the first time, the ability for clinicians to manually edit segmentation results, enhancing the tool's usability.
* **Accelerated MRI analysis time by 90%**, dramatically improving workflow efficiency for researchers.
* **Delivered a fully documented, open-source tool** that is now widely accessible to the neuroimaging community, fostering collaboration in neurodegenerative research.
